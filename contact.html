Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free on-site design consultations?

Yes, our lead architects offer an initial complimentary discovery meeting at our Gulberg Studio or directly at your site to understand your vision, take initial measurements, and outline scope.

Can I see 3D realistic views before construction starts?

Absolutely. We provide high-definition 4K photorealistic 3D visual renders showing the exact materials, paint colors, lighting, and custom furniture so you know exactly what your finished space will look like.

What are your typical project execution timelines?

Turnaround times depend on scope: Modular kitchens typically take 3 to 4 weeks. Full 1-Kanal villa turnkey execution takes between 3 to 6 months with guaranteed handover dates in our written contract.

Do you take projects outside Lahore?

Yes! We undertake turnkey architectural and interior projects in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi, Faisalabad, and Sialkot, with dedicated on-site supervisory teams.
